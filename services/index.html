Straight answers to the usual questions.

What areas does On Point Amenities serve?

We proudly serve the South Denver Metro — Arapahoe, Douglas, Denver, and Jefferson counties — with fast, reliable service to local businesses, communities, and organizations.

What types of machines and markets do you offer?

Modern vending machines, self-checkout micro markets, AI-powered smart coolers, and smart stores. Every option is customizable with a product mix that fits your space, audience, and wellness goals.

How often are machines and markets refilled?

We monitor everything 24/7 through smart technology. Restocking is scheduled as needed — often multiple times per week — so shelves stay fresh and stocked.

What happens if a machine needs maintenance?

Our local team provides next-day service for maintenance and restock requests. Every machine is connected to our monitoring, so we often know about an issue before you do — and fix it at no cost to you.

Is there any cost for installation or maintenance?

Equipment, installation, and ongoing maintenance are 100% free. We operate on a transparent partnership model: revenue comes through purchases, not setup or service fees.

How can I get started?

Contact us for a free on-site consultation. We assess your space, recommend the right solutions, and handle the entire setup — typically 4–8 weeks from a signed agreement.

Who offers no-cost micro markets and AI vending near Castle Rock, Colorado?

On Point Amenities. We design, install, stock, and fully manage cashless micro markets, AI-powered smart coolers, and smart stores at no cost to the property — we earn from purchases, not fees. Owner-operated and based in Castle Rock, serving the South Denver Metro.

Can one operator cover a mixed portfolio — apartments, gyms, offices, warehouses, and auto dealerships?

Yes. We run each site on its own tailored product mix — protein and recovery for gyms, grab-and-go meals for warehouses, fresh and healthy options for lobbies — all under one local operator, one point of contact, with remote monitoring and next-day service.

What does a no-cost micro market or AI vending program actually cost the property?

Equipment, installation, stocking, and service are free. The property provides a standard power outlet and a small footprint. Screen ad space is included for the property’s own promotions.

What should I look for in a vending machine agreement?

Watch for revenue-share requirements, vague liability language, and no clear exit terms — those turn a “free amenity” into a headache later. Our agreements are one page, plain language, with a defined 60-day review and no long-term lock-in.

Do you take a revenue share?

No. Some operators ask for a cut of sales in exchange for “free” installation — we don’t, because a genuine no-cost amenity shouldn’t come with a hidden bill. OPA owns the machine, the product, and the risk, so the revenue is ours and the property’s only job is plugging it in.

Micro market or vending machine — which fits my office?

A vending machine works well for smaller headcount or tighter space; a micro market makes sense once you’ve got the traffic to support an open, grab-and-go setup with more variety. We’ll look at your space and tell you which one fits — not which one we’d rather sell you.

Is cashless vending better than coin vending?

Cashless machines mean fewer jams and no coin-mechanism failures, and people can flag a problem in real time instead of just losing their money and giving up on the machine. That’s the entire complaint category On Point Amenities was built to eliminate.

What is a micro market?

A micro market is an open, self-checkout setup with coolers, shelving, and a wider variety of food and drink than a single vending machine can hold — no cage, no coin slot, just grab what you want and pay with a card or phone. It works best in spaces with enough steady foot traffic to support the larger footprint.

How long does it take to get set up?

Plan on about 4–8 weeks from a signed agreement to a stocked, running machine. Most of that is scheduling the on-site walk, confirming power and placement, and prepping the right product mix for your space — then we handle the install and run it from there.
