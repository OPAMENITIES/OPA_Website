Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

On Point Amenities (“we,” “us”) operates opamenities.com. This policy explains what information we collect on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain because that’s how we do business.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you submit our contact form, we receive the details you provide: name, email, phone, property name and type, city, and your message. The same applies if you email or call us directly.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — pages visited, approximate location (city level), device type, and how you found us. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

How we use it

Contact information is used for one purpose: responding to your inquiry and, if you become a client, managing our service relationship. Inquiry details are stored in our customer relationship management system so we can follow up reliably. Analytics data is used to improve the website and understand which pages are useful.

What we don’t do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not run advertising on this site, and we do not share your information with third parties for their marketing. We only disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

This site uses cookies only for the analytics described above. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies beyond that.

Data retention & your choices

We retain inquiry records for as long as needed to serve you or as required for business records. You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us — we’ll honor reasonable requests promptly.
