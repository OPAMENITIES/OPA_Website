The gym-owner FAQ.

Does this cost the gym anything?

No. Equipment, installation, stocking, and maintenance are $0. We earn when members buy — never from your budget.

Can you stock what my members actually want?

That's the whole model. We stock gym coolers like gym coolers — protein, electrolytes, recovery, clean energy — and we ask your members directly, then adjust the mix around their requests.

Will my front desk end up handling vending problems?

No. If an order ever goes wrong, the member flags it at the machine, the owner gets a real-time alert, and it's fixed the same day — usually with a personal call. Your staff never touches it.

Does it work for a 24-hour gym?

It's built for one. Cashless, AI-secured, monitored around the clock — the 5am pre-workout crowd and the 11pm finishers get the same stocked shelves, with zero staffing.

Can it carry our gym's branding?

Yes — your logo and announcements run on the machine's header screen. At Arcane Barbell in Castle Rock, the machine carries the gym's own brand and announces new products to members.

How much space does it need?

About the footprint of a household refrigerator — a lobby corner, a wall by the locker rooms, or next to the front desk. The free walkthrough finds the spot.
