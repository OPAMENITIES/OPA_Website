The property-manager FAQ.

Does this cost the property anything?

In nearly all cases, no. Equipment, installation, stocking, and maintenance are $0 to the property. We earn when residents buy — never from your budget.

How much space does it need?

A smart cooler needs about the footprint of a household refrigerator. Micro markets scale to the room. During the free walkthrough we measure and recommend the right format.

Who handles resident complaints?

We do — directly. If an order ever goes wrong, the resident can flag it at the machine and the owner gets a real-time alert. It's resolved the same day, usually with a personal call. The complaint never lands on your desk.

What if residents want different products?

That's the system working. Every machine carries a request line, and every service visit includes checking what residents are asking for. The mix evolves with your community.

How fast can we be up and running?

About four weeks from a signed agreement. The walkthrough takes twenty minutes; we handle everything after that.

What happens if it doesn't work out?

We'll be honest before it ever gets there — if your traffic or layout isn't a fit, we say so at the walkthrough instead of installing something that underperforms.
