The dealer FAQ.

Does this cost the dealership anything?

No. Equipment, installation, stocking, and maintenance are $0. We earn when people buy — never from your budget.

Can the screen really run our service specials?

Yes — that's standard, not an add-on. Oil-change packages, tire rotations, seasonal promos: your content, changeable any time. At Castle Rock Autoplex the screen runs the dealership's own service offer all day.

Who keeps it stocked and working?

We do. Machines are monitored 24/7 and restocked before they run low. Maintenance is next-day, and we usually know about an issue before your team does.

Can we cover both showroom and service lane?

Yes — new-car sales, used-car sales, service waiting, and employee breakrooms. At the Autoplex we run both sales departments; the second machine was requested by the used-car team themselves.

What does the machine look like in a showroom?

A sleek black glass-door cooler with a lit interior and a branded header screen — it reads as part of a modern showroom, not a breakroom relic.

How fast can we be running?

Typically 4–8 weeks from a signed agreement. At the Autoplex, the used-car contract was signed within 48 hours of the ask — install followed on schedule.
