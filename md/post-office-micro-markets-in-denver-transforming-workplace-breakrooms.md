## What Are Office Micro Markets in Denver?

[Micro markets](https://opamenities.com/services/) create an open-concept retail experience right in your breakroom. Employees can browse shelves and coolers stocked with fresh meals, snacks, and beverages and complete their purchase through a self-checkout kiosk.

Office micro markets in Denver provide:

- 24/7 self-service access to food and beverages
- Fresh meals, including salads and wraps
- Cashless checkout options, including mobile wallets and tap-to-pay
- Expanded product variety
- A modern breakroom solution

These markets fit well in corporate offices, coworking spaces, and business campuses looking to enhance workplace amenities with a more modern experience.
