## What On Point Amenities Offers

On Point Amenities provides a [range of modern vending solutions](https://opamenities.com/services/) and unattended retail options designed for today’s workplaces and properties. Services are intentionally benefit-focused, prioritizing convenience, variety, and ease of management.

### Modern Vending Machines

Updated vending machines go beyond traditional snack-and-soda setups. Our machines support cashless payments and a broader product mix, including better-for-you choices, while reducing many of the issues associated with older equipment. They work well in spaces where simplicity and operational efficiency are priorities.

### Micro Markets

Micro markets provide a self-serve retail experience inside offices and coworking spaces. With open shelving, coolers, and easy self-checkout, micro markets give your employees more choice and a more intuitive experience than vending. Markets work especially well in corporate environments where people want on-site convenience, flexibility, and variety.

### Smart Coolers and Smart Stores

For higher-traffic locations like residential properties, fitness centers, and hotels that need 24/7 access to refreshments, smart stores and smart coolers are a fitting solution. They provide secure access to fresh meals, snacks, and beverages, while maintaining a clean, modern look.

Across all refreshment solutions, we tailor setups and product selections to the preferences of each location’s users.
