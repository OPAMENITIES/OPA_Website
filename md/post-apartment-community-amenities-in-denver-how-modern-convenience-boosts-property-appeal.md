## How Apartment Vending Supports Residents Around the Clock

Convenient access to refreshments is one of the simplest ways to improve the resident experience. Modern apartment vending solutions provide snacks and beverages when grocery stores, restaurants, or convenience stores may be closed.

Today’s smart vending solutions offer:

- 24/7 access to snacks and beverages
- Fresh food options and healthy items
- Cashless payment options, including mobile wallets and tap-to-pay

For residents working late shifts or returning home after business hours, on-site refreshment access can be a valuable convenience.

## Smart Coolers and Smart Stores Add Secure Convenience

For apartment communities that want fresh food options while maintaining secure access, smart coolers and smart stores provide an easy grab-and-go solution. Residents present their payment method to unlock the unit, remove their items, and simply close the door to complete checkout automatically.

Both options help properties offer fresh food choices on-site, expand amenity offerings without dedicated staffing, and provide convenient 24/7 access to refreshments.

### Smart Coolers

Smart coolers use AI-powered technology that accurately detects the items removed and allows residents to access fresh meals, drinks, and snacks through convenient cashless checkout.

Their compact footprint makes them a strong fit for apartment lobbies, clubhouses, and common areas where space may be limited.

### Smart Stores

Smart stores combine the security of a smart cooler with the expanded product variety of a micro market. Residents can access a larger selection of fresh meals, beverages, snacks, and convenience items. Smart stores are also scalable, allowing up to four units to be connected through one checkout system, making them ideal for common areas with higher foot traffic.

Communities evaluating modern refreshment solutions often find that smart technology creates a premium amenity experience while maintaining security and convenience.

## Micro Markets Provide Expanded Product Variety and Flexibility

Micro markets provide another flexible option among today’s apartment community amenities, offering expanded product variety, including fresh meals, and convenient, cashless payment. These self-service, retail-style spaces include open shelving, coolers, and a self-checkout kiosk, creating a mini convenience store experience within the property.

Larger markets with multiple coolers and shelving units can support large communities and resident areas with higher foot traffic, while smaller micro markets can fit compact spaces and serve smaller resident populations. Their flexibility allows communities to select a setup that aligns with available space and resident demand.
