## Around-the-Clock Refreshment Access for Healthcare Facilities

Hospitals and medical facilities operate 24/7, while cafeterias and other food-service options have limited hours. Healthcare vending gives overnight clinical teams, patients, and visitors access to food and beverages when staffed dining options are unavailable.

Benefits of hospital vending services include:

- 24/7 access to snacks, beverages, and fresh food
- Cashless payment options with credit cards, debit cards, and mobile wallets
- Self-service purchasing without requiring another staffed food service location
- Flexible placement in waiting areas, lobbies, nurses’ lounges, and other high-use locations

## Hospital Vending Machines, Smart Coolers, and Smart Stores

For healthcare administrators, evaluating your available space, expected traffic, and desired food selection can help determine which vending service is the best fit for your healthcare facility.

### Hospital Vending Machines

Hospital vending machines offer a simple solution to provide snack and beverage access in common areas and employee breakrooms. Cashless payment options allow users to pay with credit cards or mobile wallets, making it easy for staff to purchase refreshments during short breaks and for visitors in waiting rooms to grab a beverage or snack.

### Smart Coolers

Smart coolers for hospitals expand the selection to fresh food items while remaining a secure solution with a compact footprint. Users present their payment method to unlock the cooler, grab their items, and close the door to checkout automatically.

Benefits of smart coolers include:

- AI technology that detects items removed
- Access to fresh meals and healthy snack options
- Compact footprint that works well in areas with limited space
- Secure, cashless checkout and grab-and-go convenience

### Smart Stores

Healthcare facilities that need greater capacity may opt for smart stores to create a larger unattended retail space. Smart stores use the same secure access and automatic checkout process as smart coolers while providing space for a broader selection of food and beverages.

With scalable options that can connect up to four units through one checkout system, smart stores are ideal for larger facilities and high-traffic locations.
