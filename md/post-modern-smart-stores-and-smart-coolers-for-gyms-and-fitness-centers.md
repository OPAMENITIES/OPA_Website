## Choosing the Right Refreshment Solution

The right equipment depends on the products you want to offer, available space, and member traffic. On Point Amenities provides vending machines, smart coolers, and smart stores that can be tailored to the needs of your facility.

### Vending Machines

Modern gym vending machines provide a dependable solution for snacks and beverages. Cashless payment options make it easy for members to grab a healthy snack or cold beverage during and after a workout.

### Smart Coolers

For facilities that want to expand product variety, smart coolers for gyms provide secure access to fresh food and beverages within a compact footprint.

Members present their payment method to unlock the cooler, select their items, and close the door to complete checkout automatically. Smart coolers have a small footprint making them ideal for lobbies, locker areas, and other areas with limited space.

### Smart Stores

Smart stores are self-service units that offer the same secure access as smart coolers while offering a larger product selection. As a scalable solution, up to four connected units can operate through a single checkout experience, making smart stores ideal for larger fitness centers and high-traffic areas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What products can be stocked in a gym vending machine?**

Gym vending machines can be stocked with bottled water, sports drinks, electrolyte beverages, protein bars, granola bars, trail mix, and other grab-and-go snacks. On Point Amenities works with each fitness center to build a product selection that reflects member preferences and purchasing habits.

**Are smart coolers a good fit for smaller fitness centers?**

Yes, smart coolers have a compact footprint, making them a great option for fitness centers with limited space. Smart coolers allow facilities to offer fresh meals, protein drinks, yogurt, and other refrigerated items without requiring a large area.

**How do smart coolers and smart stores work?**

Members unlock the smart coolers and smart stores by presenting a payment method, grabbing the items they want, and closing the door to complete purchase automatically. This provides a fast, cashless purchasing experience while maintaining secure access.
